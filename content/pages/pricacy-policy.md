      # Multi-Tiered Privacy Policy


      **Effective Date:** 3 Mar 2025

      **Last Updated:** 3 Mar 2025


      Welcome to Multi-Tiered. We respect your privacy and are committed to
      protecting your personal information across all our applications,
      software, and games. This Privacy Policy explains what information we
      collect, how we use it, and the choices you have regarding your
      information.


      ## 1. Overview


      By installing, using, or accessing any of our applications or services,
      you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with
      our practices, please do not use our services and remove any related
      applications from your device.


      ## 2. Information We Collect


      **Personal Information:**


      *   **Account Information:** When you sign up or log in, we may collect
      your email address, username, and other details necessary for account
      creation and authentication.


      *   **Usage Data:** We automatically collect data on how you interact with
      our apps (e.g., features used, time spent, device information).


      *   **Ad Interactions:** Our applications may use third-party advertising
      partners that collect certain data for ad personalization.


      **Non-Personal Information:**


      *   We may gather anonymous usage statistics, crash logs, and performance
      metrics to help us improve our services.


      ## 3. How We Use Your Information


      *   **To Provide and Improve Our Services:** Your data helps us maintain,
      enhance, and personalize our applications.


      *   **For Communication:** We may send you emails regarding account
      updates, service changes, or important notices.


      *   **For Advertising:** We work with trusted third-party ad networks that
      may use your data to deliver personalized ads. All such processing
      complies with applicable privacy laws.


      *   **For Analytics:** Usage data helps us understand trends and usage
      patterns so we can improve user experience.


      ## 4. Data Sharing and Disclosure


      *   **Third-Party Services:** We share data only with third-party partners
      (such as advertising networks and analytics providers) who adhere to
      strict data protection standards.


      *   **Legal Requirements:** We may disclose your information if required
      by law or to protect our rights, property, or safety.


      *   **User Consent:** In cases where we intend to share data beyond these
      scopes, we will request your consent where required by law.


      ## 5. User Rights and Account Management


      *   **Access and Correction:** You have the right to review and update
      your personal information through your account settings.


      *   **Account Deletion:** Our applications include a feature that allows
      you to delete your account. Upon deletion, we will remove your personal
      information in accordance with applicable laws.


      *   **Opt-Out:** You may opt-out of certain data collection or
      communications by following instructions provided within our apps or by
      contacting us.


      ## 6. Data Security


      We take data security seriously and implement industry-standard measures
      to protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission
      over the Internet is 100% secure. Please take care to protect your account
      credentials.


      ## 7. Children’s Privacy


      Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 13 (or the
      applicable age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal
      data from children. If you believe that a child’s information has been
      inadvertently collected, please contact us immediately.


      ## 8. Changes to This Policy


      Multi-Tiered may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes
      will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage
      you to review our Privacy Policy periodically.


      ## 9. Contact Us


      If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy
      Policy, please contact us at:


      *   **Email:** [multitiered696@gmail.com]()


      *   **Alternate Email:** [multi-tiered@protonmail.com]()
